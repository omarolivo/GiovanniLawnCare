Why Seasonal Cleanups Are Vital

Seasonal cleanups do more than make your yard look neat. Thick mats of wet fallen leaves block sunlight and trap moisture against grass crowns. Over the late autumn and winter months, this moisture breeds fungal spores like snow mold, rots delicate turf roots, and creates dead spots that require expensive overseeding in spring.

At Giovanni Lawn Care LLC, we don’t just blow leaves into disorganized woodland edges. We deploy heavy-duty commercial truck-mounted leaf loaders that vacuum, shred, and haul away huge volumes of yard waste from your property completely.
