Gardens and Lawns Built with Pride

We understand that your property is one of your greatest investments, and we work closely with you from start to finish to bring your outdoor vision to life. Whether you need complete new construction landscaping or a thoughtful seasonal facelift for existing tired garden beds, our experienced crew focuses on the smallest details.

Connecticut Shoreline properties face specific challenges—sandy soils, salt air, and varying shade patterns. We select plants and turf varieties that naturally flourish in our unique microclimates.

Services Included in Our Lawn & Garden Care

Planting & Garden Bed Design

Colorful perennials, ornamental grasses, coastal-hardy shrubs, flowering hydrangeas, and specimen trees selected for year-round beauty.

Fresh Bark Mulching & Crisp Bed Edging

Deep spade machine edging that creates distinct definition between turf and beds, topped with premium natural dark bark mulch that suppresses weeds and retains soil moisture.

Sod Turf Installation

Transform bare dirt or patchy grass into an instant, lush emerald lawn in a single day. We grade the soil, enrich with loam, and lay nursery-grown sod seamlessly.

Coastal Hydroseeding

Cost-effective, rapid germination slurry of certified grass seed, mulch fibers, and fertilizer ideal for new construction yards, slopes, and large lawn renovations.

Shrub Trimming & Ornamental Pruning

Keeping hedges tight, foundation shrubs properly sized away from siding, and ornamental perennials deadheaded and vigorous.
