* **Why Shoreline Properties Choose Mechanical Beach Cleaning:**  
  * **Safe for Bare Feet:** Sifts out broken glass, sharp oyster/clam shells, small rocks, metal fragments, and litter hidden in the top layer of sand.  
  * **Odor & Insect Prevention:** Removes heavy piles of rotting kelp and seaweed wrack before they ferment in the summer sun and attract flies and gnats.  
  * **Eco-Conscious Dune Protection:** Low-impact mechanical sifting cleans the sand while respecting natural dune grass, coastal berms, and shoreline habitats.  
* **Our Specialized Equipment — The Barber Surf Rake:**  
  * Towed behind our specialized coastal tractor, the **Barber Surf Rake** mechanically screens and sifts the upper inches of sand, trapping debris in a collection hopper while leaving clean, aerated sand behind.  
* **What Our Beach Cleaning Service Includes:**  
  1. **Mechanical Sand Sifting & Grooming:** Screening buried stones, shells, and litter with a resort-quality smooth finish.  
  2. **Seaweed & Kelp Removal:** Hauling away heavy tidal wrack.  
  3. **Post-Storm Flotsam Clearing:** Fast mobilization after Nor'easters or extreme high tides to clear driftwood, timber, and washed-up debris.  
  4. **Roll-Off Dumpster Support:** Because Giovanni Lawn Care owns and operates roll-off dumpsters, we can immediately haul away massive loads of storm debris without waiting on a third-party hauler.  
  5. **Flexible Scheduling:** Available for recurring weekly/bi-weekly association maintenance, seasonal spring beach opening, or one-time grooming before coastal weddings and family events.  
* **How Our 4-Step Beach Service Works:**  
  * **Step 01 — Shoreline Assessment:** We check your beach access, sand condition, tide lines, and debris volume.  
  * **Step 02 — Custom Maintenance Plan:** Choose weekly/bi-weekly grooming, post-storm response, or pre-event cleaning.  
  * **Step 03 — Low-Tide Sifting & Clearing:** Our uniformed crew arrives during optimal low-tide windows with our Barber Surf Rake.
