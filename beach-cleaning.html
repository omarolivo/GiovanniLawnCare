State-of-the-Art Sand Grooming

Velvet-Smooth, Barefoot-Safe Waterfronts

Connecticut’s coastline is known for dramatic tides, beautiful sandy beaches, and unfortunately, heavy tidal wrack. After high winds, Nor'easters, or normal seasonal changes, beaches collect piles of decaying kelp, sharp clam shells, glass fragments, driftwood, and hidden debris that make walking unpleasant and unsafe.

Giovanni Lawn Care LLC is proud to bring industrial-grade mechanical beach grooming to private waterfront homeowners, beach associations, yacht clubs, and coastal resorts using our John Deere tractor and specialized Barber Surf Rake equipment.

Why Choose Mechanical Beach Cleaning?

Safety

Safe for Bare Feet

Mechanically sifts out broken bottles, razor-sharp oyster shells, jagged rocks, and hidden flotsam buried in the top several inches of sand.

Clean

Odor & Insect Prevention

Removes thick layers of decaying seaweed and kelp wrack before it rots in the humid summer sun and breeds swarms of gnats and flies.

Eco

Eco-Conscious Dune Protection

Low-impact pneumatic turf tires and fine sifting tines ensure we clean the sand without tearing up coastal berms, natural dune grasses, or fragile shoreline environments.

Haul

In-House Dumpster Haul-Away

Because Giovanni Lawn Care owns roll-off dumpsters and hooklift trucks, we don't dump dirty seaweed in unsightly heaps on your grass. We load it and haul it off your property immediately.

How Our 4-Step Beach Service Works

Step 1

Shoreline Assessment

We inspect beach access ramps, sand depth, high-tide markers, and debris density to recommend the ideal grooming schedule.

Step 2

Custom Maintenance Plan

Choose recurring weekly or bi-weekly grooming for associations, seasonal Memorial Day beach opening, or pre-event wedding grooming.

Step 3

Low-Tide Sifting & Clearing

Our experienced operator coordinates around low-tide windows with the Barber Surf Rake to maximize clean beach acreage.

Step 4

Responsible Haul-Away

All collected wrack, dead kelp, and trash are cleanly removed into our dumpsters—leaving nothing behind except pristine combed sand.
