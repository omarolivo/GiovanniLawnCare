The Giovanni Difference — Established 2010

Built on hard work. Known for good work.

"Giovanni Lawn Care was established in 2010 by its owner, Giovanni Sanchez, who came from very humble beginnings. Giovanni started in the landscape industry during his teenage years and continued as a young adult to earn extra money while working a full-time manufacturing job."

"When he lost his manufacturing job due to the economy crashing, Giovanni found himself working several jobs to make ends meet. Giovanni began to think of what he could do to earn a living—something he felt passionate about that would allow him to provide for his growing family. He decided to invest what little he had in a lawn mower, a trailer, and a few power tools—this was just the beginning."

"He worked evenings and weekends around his full-time shifts, working 12 or more hours a day, 7 days a week. Phone calls started pouring in. Today, with over 20 years of hands-on experience, Giovanni’s main focus is to deliver quality work, exceptional service, and green lawns across the Connecticut Shoreline."

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services, estimates, crew, equipment, and dumpster policies.

All work is performed by our well-trained, supervised crew using commercial-grade equipment. Every team member wears an official Giovanni Lawn Care company uniform so you always know who is on your property.

Yes! We care for single-family homes, private beach associations, HOA communities, and commercial business properties across the Connecticut Shoreline.

We offer both weekly and bi-weekly mowing. We strongly recommend weekly mowing during the main growing season because it keeps grass healthier and prevents clumping, uneven cuts, and lawn fungus.

Every yard, driveway, and stonework project is unique in size, access, terrain, and materials required. Instead of guessing with one-size-fits-all pricing, we provide fast, free, no-obligation quotes tailored to your exact property.

We offer 12, 15, 20, and 25 cubic yard roll-off containers to accommodate every residential project, yard cleanup, or commercial job site across the Connecticut Shoreline. All dumpsters feature ground-level rear walk-in doors and are delivered using our driveway-safe hooklift trucks. Containers are ideal for yard waste, storm brush, branches, estate decluttering, and construction or remodeling debris. Because state regulations restrict hazardous materials (such as wet paint, hazardous chemicals, liquids, and tires), please mention your debris when calling (860) 662-6293 and we will confirm all loading guidelines upfront.
