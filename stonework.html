The Backbone of a Great Connecticut Landscape

Patios, walkways, and stone walls—known as hardscaping—are the architectural foundation of your property. When designed thoughtfully and built with rigorous base preparation, they function like an outdoor room added directly onto your home, increasing property value and extending your summer living space.

At Giovanni Lawn Care LLC, we don’t cut corners on base preparation. A patio is only as strong as what lies underneath. We excavate properly, install geotextile fabric, lay down heavy layers of crushed gravel, and machine-compact every single layer with industrial vibratory plate compactors to eliminate settling, shifting, or frost heave during cold Connecticut winters.
